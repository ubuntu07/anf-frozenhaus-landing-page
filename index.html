Our story

From a small freezer to Bukidnon's partner

ANF Frozenhaus started with a simple idea: give local food businesses a reliable source of quality frozen products without the hassle of long trips to the city. What began as a single freezer in Malaybalay has grown into a trusted supplier for carenderias, milk tea shops, and resellers across Bukidnon.

Today we operate two branches and stock everything from tempura and siomai to InJoy beverage powders — all kept at the right temperature from delivery to your kitchen.

2019 The first freezer Opened our first stall in Malaybalay with a small frozen snack lineup.
2021 Milk tea line added Partnered with InJoy to supply powders for the growing milk tea scene.
2023 Valencia branch opens Expanded to a second location along Sayre Highway to serve more partners.
Today 40+ products in stock Supporting restaurants, resellers, and home cooks across the province.

FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers before you order — for anything else, our chatbot is right here.

Do you accept bulk or reseller orders?
Yes. We welcome resellers and food businesses. Message us on Facebook or open the chat to discuss reseller pricing and minimum order quantities.
How do I get the latest price list?
For the latest price list, visit either branch or send us a message on Facebook (facebook.com/anffrozenhaus). We're happy to send a quote for bulk orders.
Do you deliver?
Delivery and pickup arrangements depend on your area. Message us on Facebook so we can confirm availability and logistics before your order.
What payment methods do you accept?
Please message us on Facebook to confirm accepted payment methods and terms — especially for bulk and reseller orders.
